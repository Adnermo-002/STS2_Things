Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: A single continuous vertical background plate for the Slay the Spire 2 act map, for a new underground region called the Depths.
Reference image: The provided original Hive map parchment is ONLY a reference for the game's restrained hand-painted UI style, broad quiet center and narrow irregular outer margins. Create an entirely new STONE slab, not parchment. Do not copy its stains or shapes.
Primary request: One very tall, vertically continuous, ancient pale blue-grey limestone tablet seen perfectly straight-on. This is the surface onto which a route map will later be drawn by the game. It should feel like a single long excavated stone slab, with broad hand-chiseled bevels, a few uneven edge chips, faint mineral veins and very subtle surface wear. No horizontal panels, dividers, stacked tablets or grid of tiles. The central 70% of the width is smooth, calm, pale and light enough for small dark slate map symbols and dotted route lines to remain extremely readable. The center should still read as matte stone, not paper, clouds or a flat blur.
Shape: Very tall portrait slab filling about 88% of the image width and 96% height. Its left and right silhouettes are roughly vertical but hand-hewn and naturally chipped, never ruler straight. Softly broken stone shoulders at the top and a low irregular stone foot at the bottom. All side-edge material continues naturally all the way from top to bottom. No seams at one-third or two-thirds height.
Decoration: At the outermost side margins only, sparse desaturated teal mineral blooms, hairline cracks and a few simple dark-blue crystal inclusions, with very restrained moss caught in small chips. Decoration occupies less than 15% of width on either side. No prominent central emblems, carvings, runes, faces, skulls or glowing cracks. No rendered environment behind it.
Style: Match original Slay the Spire 2's simple stylized hand-painted map assets. Broad opaque painted planes, slightly irregular edges, subtle gouache grain, soft diffuse lighting, limited color palette. A few simplified planar stone facets around the edges. Flat orthographic 2D game UI asset. NOT a photograph, not a realistic PBR texture, not a glossy 3D render or isometric floor. Avoid ornate fantasy picture frames.
Palette and readability: The main face is pale neutral slate with a faint cool blue-green cast (roughly RGB 175–195). Side bevels are muted grey-blue, never near black. Highlights are subdued chalk. Keep center contrast low and color stable over the full length, with no horizontal lighting bands or vertical spotlight gradient.
Composition: One uninterrupted full-length image. No map paths, no dots, no locations, no icons, no typography, no labels, no border of the image itself, no watermark. Leave a narrow flat PURE bright green #00FF00 margin outside the stone silhouette for chroma removal. No shadow or halo outside the silhouette. The stone itself contains no bright green.
