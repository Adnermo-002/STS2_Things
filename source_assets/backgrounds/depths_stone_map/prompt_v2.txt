Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: A distinctive STONE MAP BACKGROUND for the Depths, a Slay the Spire 2 underground act.
Reference image: An original Slay the Spire 2 map background, shown at the required long portrait proportions. Use its simple flat graphic language, broad quiet drawing area and approximate vertical silhouette only. Paint a completely new fossil-stone design. Do not copy parchment details.
REVISION PRIORITY: The previous attempt looked like a generic, over-detailed realistic stone frame. This revision must be MUCH SIMPLER and have a strong original identity. Use four or five broad flat painted color regions, large cartoon shapes and only a few confident brush strokes. At thumbnail size it should still be instantly recognizable. No mottled realism, grain, flecks, little scratches, tiny cracks, glittering crystals or generic RPG ornamental frame.
Concept: THE DROWNED FOSSIL TABLET. One tall asymmetric slab of pale blue-green stone excavated from the bottom of an ancient cave. Its edges reveal gigantic, charmingly simplified sea-creature fossils: one large naive curled ammonite shape partly embedded in the upper LEFT rim, and one chunky stylized fish fossil partly embedded in the lower RIGHT rim, made of a blunt head, a bold spine and just three broad ribs. These are simple stone relief SHAPES, not detailed anatomical drawings or separate floating objects. A few large broken steps and two broad geological faults along the margins give the slab a memorable silhouette. The slab should feel a little strange and playful, like original Slay the Spire 2 environments.
Layout: Exactly one uninterrupted long portrait plate, near a 1:2.12 width-to-height ratio. Its middle 65–70% width is one continuous pale matte stone face, essentially calm flat blue-grey, with only two or three very subtle broad tone shifts. All recognizable fossil forms and deep shadow shapes stay confined to the outer left/right 15% bands. Keep the center completely clear for dark route icons. The top and bottom are unevenly broken caps on the same continuous stone, not separate panels. No horizontal banded sections or framing inside the surface.
Palette: pale blue-green stone face, medium muted teal-grey side planes, a few dark desaturated navy cuts only at the very outside edge, a restrained warm ochre fossil accent. Four to five dominant colors, flat opaque gouache. The center needs a light value near RGB 175–190 for readability.
Drawing style: Simplified 2D cartoon hand-painted game UI, original Slay the Spire 2 sensibility, broad slightly crooked shapes, clean readable edges, economical intentional brushwork. Planar and graphic, almost like a cut-paper silhouette painted in gouache. NO photorealism, NO smooth 3D bevel rendering, NO busy mineral texture. Absolutely no fine texture or intricate decorative etching.
Composition: straight-on orthographic view. One connected complete slab, entirely in frame, on a uniform bright-green chroma background outside the silhouette. No cast shadow, haze, glow, environment, text, map nodes, route paths, letters, symbols, grid, multiple variants or watermark.
