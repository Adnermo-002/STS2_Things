# Focused arm family candidate v01 — REJECTED

The focused board preserves the desired matte palette but repeats the structural failure:

- upper-arm pieces terminate in visible circular cuffs and black cavities;
- shoulder plates contain attached cloth plugs instead of being independent plates;
- forearm pieces terminate in flat dark sockets;
- hands include wrist hardware/fused cuff geometry;
- near and far chains are not faithful bind-pose counterparts of the locked master.

The board is retained only as rendering reference. No attachment is approved for the production rig.
