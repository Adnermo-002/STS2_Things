# Thief Raider v11 — Visual Gate Rejected

Date: 2026-07-16

v11 is closed and must not be exported, packed, installed, or used as a donor for production attachments.

## Why it failed

- `near_arm_mesh_v01/attack_contact_arm_checker_zoom.jpg` exposes a torn shoulder, a broken elbow silhouette, and isolated dark pixels.
- `body_bend_v01/*_waist_checker_zoom.jpg` exposes transparent gaps through the waist, far arm, strap, cape, and shoulder chain.
- The contact sheets show rigid sticker rotation rather than a connected shoulder–elbow–wrist deformation chain.
- `semantic_atlas_candidate_v01_alpha.png` contains hollow sleeve openings, circular plug ends, composite torso/armor pieces, and inconsistent joint engineering.
- Exact RGBA reconstruction at bind pose only proves the static image was copied; it does not prove that the parts form an animatable character.

## Forbidden reuse

- No v11 mesh, underpaint, generated sleeve, torso attachment, or animation frame may enter the shipping atlas.
- The locked full-character master remains the identity/color reference only.
- The existing Steam package remains untouched until a new candidate passes full-motion and in-game layer gates.
