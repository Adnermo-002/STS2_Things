## Structural constraints

- Each limb piece represents only one bone segment.
- Upper arm ends before the elbow; forearm begins at the elbow and ends before the wrist.
- Thigh ends before the knee; lower leg begins at the knee and ends before the ankle.
- No shoulder plate, bracer, belt, pouch, boot, dagger, rope, scarf, hood, or cape is fused to these pieces.
- Both ends are painted solid and tapered with 20–30% hidden overlap. There is no visible tube opening or joint socket.
- Maintain left-facing three-quarter perspective, short compact Raider proportions, original palette, and smooth matte rendering.
