# VISUAL GATE REJECTED — thief_raider_v12

Rejected after direct visual review. Do not promote any v12 texture, rig, animation, PCK, or Steam asset.

## Blocking failures
- Generated underbody changes the locked character's silhouette, proportions, face/hood mass, and painterly language.
- Accessories are pasted onto a different body rather than forming one coherent character.
- Rope floats as a disconnected U loop; scarf, shoulder plates, sack, and dagger do not share believable contact/occlusion.
- Dagger grip is anatomically inconsistent and weapon/hand layer ownership is wrong.
- Attack and hurt expose torn triangular shoulder/elbow/waist seams.
- Death is a rigid root rotation, clips outside the QA canvas, and never settles on the ground.
- Static bind similarity and pixel-coverage checks did not predict animation quality.

## Disposition
Archive as failed research only. Restart from the locked master using full semantic meshes (continuous arms/legs) plus generated hidden underpaint, then gate every motion on checkerboard and combat-scale previews.
