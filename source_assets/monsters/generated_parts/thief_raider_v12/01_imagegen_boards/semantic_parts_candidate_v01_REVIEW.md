# Semantic Parts Candidate v01 — Donor Board Only

The board is a clear improvement over v11 in palette, silhouette smoothness, spacing, and accessory quality, but it does not pass the production attachment gate.

## Usable donors

- hood and eyes
- shoulder and forearm armor plates
- belt, pouch, sack, rope and knot
- both cape tails
- both separate boots
- dagger

## Failed production requirements

- Torso includes both arms and exposes a hollow neck port.
- Both scarf candidates are closed hollow tubes rather than front/back cloth layers.
- Three arm candidates fuse cloth, leather and metal into one rigid piece.
- The dagger hand includes the weapon and the rope hand includes rope/cuff geometry.
- All large leg candidates fuse two or three joints, so the hip, knee and ankle cannot articulate independently.
- No complete independent near/far upper-arm, forearm, thigh and shin set exists.

The board remains a visual donor. It must not be converted directly into a Spine atlas.
